Frequently Asked Questions 💡

Is Urlify.io completely free to use? ▼

Yes, basic URL extraction and standard media format conversions are completely free for all users without any registration required.

Are the generated public URLs permanent? ▼

All generated asset links are permanent and remain active indefinitely, making them ideal for blogs, documentation, and websites.

How secure are my uploaded files? ▼

All uploaded files are processed securely through encrypted cloud channels and deleted automatically to ensure maximum user privacy.

Do I need to sign in to use the tools? ▼

Basic extractions are open to everyone, but signing in via Google helps you track your upload history and manage your links easily.

What file formats are supported? ▼

We support a wide range of formats including JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, MP4, PDF, and Excel documents.

What is the maximum file upload size? ▼

Free users can upload files up to 2MB to 10MB, while Pro subscribers enjoy higher limits up to 50MB+ per file.

Can I use generated URLs in HTML tags? ▼

Absolutely! You can copy the generated direct links and paste them directly into standard HTML img tags, markdown docs, or emails.

How does the AI Automation Review work? ▼

Our AI scans your uploaded assets to analyze metadata, optimize formats, and provide smart feedback ratings after task completion.
